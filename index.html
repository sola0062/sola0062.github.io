Final project · Mobile app

Glow-Up

A fitness and nutrition app for university students. It brings workouts, meal logging and progress tracking into one dark, focused interface built around progress rings and a four-tab navigation: Home, Train, Fuel and Glow.

Role
Solo designer: research, flows, UI, prototype
Tools
Figma, AI-assisted design (a requirement of the brief)
Scope
9 connected mobile screens
  • Designed onboarding: splash screen and a university-email login.
  • Built a home dashboard with daily goal progress, streaks and remaining calories.
  • Designed a workout library, a live workout timer, and a routine builder.
  • Added meal logging with macro balance, plus a profile with milestones and a consistency heatmap.
  • Kept one type scale, one cyan accent and one card style across every screen.
